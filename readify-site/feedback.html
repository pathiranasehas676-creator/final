FAQ

How do I save my reading list?

Use the “Save to Reading List” buttons in Explorer or Recommender.

Can I access Readify offline?

Yes, the PWA caches pages after your first visit.

How often does Author of the Day change?

It updates daily using the system date.

Can I reset my progress data?

Clear your browser localStorage to reset saved data.

Is Readify beginner-friendly?

Yes! The code is simple and commented for viva discussions.
